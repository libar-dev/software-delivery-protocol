## 4. Design truths that live outside the graph here

The placement decision supplies most needed homes without a new primitive or relation. It does not make every work record a Spec.

| Outside record | Durable truth it may carry | Ruled graph home | What blocks or limits moving it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **OD register: sources and unresolved questions** | An unsettled choice affecting a promise. | The carrying Spec’s open question; keyed address for reference. | Most Spec-question rows already point there. The remaining duplication is provisional summaries, authority and work status. A register question that blocks a unit need not block readiness. |
| **OD register: decided tradeoffs and refusals** | Chosen option, rejected alternatives, costs and consequences. | `decision`-kind Spec, reached through `decidedBy`; later reversal through `supersedes` where applicable. | Requires classification and exact authoring. Some OD rows are headings, duplicate pointers, tactical choices or evidence, not semantic decisions. No engine change is required. |
| **OD register: `blocks`, advisor, class, checkedBy, dates and patch status** | Unit scheduling, authority and review history. | No universal canonical graph home for this work state. | MD-33 excludes scheduling authority; MD-7 excludes approval facts. Keep an external register and join it for review. |
| **Forks** | Alternatives and a durable owner ruling. | Decision Spec’s context, decision, rationale, alternatives and consequences; affected Specs link by `decidedBy`. | The fork also contains draft authorship, citation-check notes, date and sitting history. Those portions remain lineage evidence. The current tool does not perform this semantic split. |
| **Consensus ledger and r3 archives** | Findings about a guarantee, a missing dependency, a wrong design or incomplete implementation. | Correct the carrying Spec; unresolved durable choice becomes its question; a ruled tradeoff becomes a decision Spec. | Finding status, reviewer verdict and consensus are work records. Pure Design Review stores no canonical findings, and source bindings cannot prove approval. Preserve the finished r3 records. |
| **STATE tactical decisions** | Some are lasting technical policy; others are instructions about this unit. | Lasting tradeoff: decision Spec. Already-owned guarantee: ordinary revision to its carrying Spec. Generic advisory planning rule: graph-first planning Spec or recipe. | They need sorting. “npm,” a type/module rule, owner authority, lane isolation and one-off job setup are not the same truth category. Session-specific routing stays in STATE/SESSIONS. |
| **STATE leads** | Deferred behavior, re-entry condition, evidence still needed, or merely a suspected gap. | Deferred Spec’s own blocking question; `dependsOn` for a true semantic prerequisite; existing carrying Spec for a known correction. | A lead is not yet intended truth. It must first be checked and ruled. Resolved and retracted leads should not generate new Specs. |
| **ROADMAP needs-first links** | Some express actual design prerequisites; others express “do this sitting/build first.” | Semantic prerequisite: `dependsOn`; decision gate: `decidedBy` to a decision Spec; re-entry: the deferred Spec’s question. | Round, lane, size, sequence and session dependency are scheduling. | **PLAN §10 E-register** | Where the owner’s doc leaves a platform choice open. | Question on the carrying Spec; decision Spec when ruled. | Historical E aliases and doc attribution still serve local provenance. | **PLAN §11 ambiguities** | Language, structural and product questions with provisional readings. | Carrying Spec question; chosen tradeoff in decision Spec; settled guarantee on its existing Spec. | Several have already been ruled but remain in the initial plan. | **Pair inventory and emitter gaps** | A design/code comparison intention, or an unpinned design dependency. | The actual design shape belongs in the carrying Spec; absence of a necessary declaration is a design gap. | Pair states, erased parameters, implementation symbol mappings and selected compiler results remain adapter/test context. They must not become authored delivery facts. |
| **Pass history** | What a reviewer read and what changed afterward. | Durable outcomes go to Specs/questions/decisions. | The pass itself is Git/work context. A comparison wrapper can display it without adding approval primitives. |

The exact ruling is [`planning-truths-placement.sdp.md:18`](/Users/darkomijic/dev-libar/software-delivery-protocol/specs/decisions/planning-truths-placement.sdp.md:18). The register’s schema shows why wholesale migration would be wrong. Its 18 fields mix semantic references with work authority and scheduling. Its sources are 159 Spec questions, 54 owner-queue items, 51 extensions, 17 Spec lines, 12 plan ambiguities and three product decisions. It also has 46 `possiblySame` groups requiring human reconciliation. The lawful move is to remove duplicate semantic truth while retaining a smaller operational index.
