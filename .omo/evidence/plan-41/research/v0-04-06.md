`ready`, as an open question, or as a named deferral, without realization), **departed** (a decision Spec, or a
ready Spec carrying a ruling, says otherwise), **uncaptured** (no Spec states it and nothing rules it out),
**out-of-scope** (v0 deferred it, or it is framework-specific). Relevance is to design management: designs ahead
of code, iteration, review, design-versus-realization tracking, decisions and open questions, graph reading by
agents. New captures are proposed at `idea` or `scoped` only.

## Top recommendations

Ranked by value to design management, then closeness to v0.

1. **Compute example-space coverage now, without the Studio.** v0 04 §4 and 06 §8. `witness` and `coverage gap`
   are ratified terms, yet nothing computes them; the only home is the Studio coverage grid, held behind two
   blocking questions. A reader join or recipe that enumerates a parent's literal-union slots, marks each
   combination an example's bound point witnesses, and lists the missing ones gives a design its missing
   examples before code exists, and gives an agent the permutations to propose. 44 parents here own an example
   space (52 literal-union slots); the adopter has 18 (89 slots). Home: `spec:consumers.agent-surface.example-space-coverage`
   (behavior, idea), refining `spec:consumers.agent-surface`. No ruling conflicts: the harness path (plan 27, O4)
   was never refused.
2. **Review perspectives over a Pack.** v0 04 §6. The adopter iterates designs through four advisor lenses
   (`design/advisors/lenses/{convex,domain,operator,product}.md`, `task-pass.md`) by hand, and its feedback asks
   for "a review lens as a filter over the same capability" (feedback 02, items 28 and 29). `CONTEXT.md` already
   names a discipline as "a lens/filter over the graph". A corpus-owned perspective would order and filter a
   Pack's questions, entries, constraints and examples into a disposable reading brief, with no new kind and no
   stage. Home: `spec:consumers.discipline-view` (behavior, idea), refining `spec:consumers.projections-model`.
   Flag the term: "lens" already names the Studio's graph views. No conflict: a new projection does not reopen
   MD-32.
3. **Warn on a Spec shaped by a superseded decision.** v0 06 §6.3.4. Decision lineage is design management, and
   the self-hosted corpus already holds six such edges: `spec:consumers.spec-studio.lenses` is `decidedBy` MD-34,
   which MD-38 replaced (its `#architectureVocabulary` question is now answered), and five consumer Specs are
   `decidedBy` the projections freeze, which `spec:decisions.question-key-rendering` "supersedes" while saying

## Departures worth re-opening

- **A. Architecture constraints as Protocol findings (MD-38 consequence; v0 06 §6.4).** MD-38 brought back the
  data v0 checked (layer, context, uses) and kept the check out. The adopter needed it on day one: R6 of plan 40
  asks `check.py` to fail when a `platform` component, or a member of one, uses a unit in another context. A
  check over declared `uses` and declared layers infers nothing and judges no content; it is conformance of
  anchored structure to declared intent.
- **B. Two check families only, corpus rules in the corpus's gate (`spec:validation.two-check-families`, MD-38,
  plan 40 R3; v0 06 §6.5).** The adopter's gate grew to about ten graph-shaped rules, and parses carriers by regex
  to do it. Hosting corpus rules on the one validation path keeps them graph-only and puts their findings in the
  same report and Design Review. The guardrail survives if the Protocol never authors those rules.
- **C. No design rung (D1 in `spec:model.core-model`, the typing law MD-11, the kind-conditional floor MD-12;
  v0 06 §6.3.2 `designed`).** The adopter's owner asked for Specs "at any level of maturity, including a Spec whose
  key abstractions are designed as stubs"; the adopter holds 977 Design entries in 59 Specs, and all 15 members of
  `pack:history-view` state `defined`. Try recommendation 7 first. If a derived signal cannot separate designed
  from undesigned Specs, `spec:extraction.contract-declarations` (defined) is the first typed step toward a floor
  clause that reads design evidence.
- **D. No component declared ahead of code (MD-38 retained limitation; v0 03 §2.5, 04 §7 `/arch`).** A design
  ahead of code cannot name the components it will add except in Spec prose, and the Studio's architecture lens
  can draw only anchored components. The evidence is weaker than A to C: the adopter's designs so far name new
  components in Design entries. Worth re-opening alongside A, because an architecture constraint about a planned
  component needs that component to exist in the graph.
- **E. No open questions in the Gherkin carrier (`spec:carrier.gherkin-authoring`; v0 04 §3.2).** Open questions
  carry design iteration (the adopter keys 166 of them), so a Gherkin-carried behavior must move to Markdown to
  hold one. Low priority: no adopter authors Gherkin yet.

Held, and not worth re-opening on this evidence: the patch loop (the adopter's feedback asks to keep generated
status out of authored Specs and to route change through agents), decorators, Spec-side binding lists, and test
runs as graph facts.
