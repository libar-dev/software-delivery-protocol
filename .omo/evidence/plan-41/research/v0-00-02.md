# v0 chapters 00 to 02 against the current Protocol: a gap map

Scope: `docs/lineage/v0-design/00-vision-and-product.md`, `01-core-primitives.md` and
`02-system-architecture.md`, read claim by claim against the corpus and engine on
`feature/design-management-v0` (tree equal to `main` at `822aea4`), 2026-10-10. Every Spec state
below was read from the graph (`pnpm --silent sdp:q`), and the carrying Spec was then read.
"Uncaptured" was checked with at least two concept searches and a grep of `specs/`, `src/` and
`docs/concept/`. Counts are measured, not inherited: re-run the queries rather than quoting them.

Status words:

- **realized**: a Spec states it and the engine realizes it.
- **captured-ahead**: a Spec states it below `ready`, or without an implementation.
- **departed**: a decision Spec, or a ruling Spec the corpus treats as law, rules otherwise.
  Where the ruling sits only in a `defined` Spec or a concept document, the home column says so,
  because re-opening it then needs a new decision, not a supersession.
- **uncaptured**: no Spec states it and nothing rules it out.
- **out-of-scope**: v0 itself deferred it, or it is runtime-specific with no bearing on the
  Protocol.

The map holds 181 claims: 92 realized, 16 captured-ahead, 45 departed, 17 uncaptured, 11
out-of-scope (chapter 00: 22, 3, 10, 2, 5; chapter 01: 42, 7, 21, 10, 2; chapter 02: 28, 6, 14,
5, 4). The weight of the gaps sits in chapter 01: the `designed` rung, open-question metadata,
relation reasons, and the design and ui details v0 typed.

Relevance (high, medium, low) is given for uncaptured and partly captured rows only. It is
relevance to design management: holding designs ahead of code, iterating, reviewing, tracking
design against realization, decisions and open questions, and agents reading the graph.

## Top recommendations

Ranked by value to design management first and closeness to v0 second. None needs a `ready`
decision Spec superseded; the ones that touch a law name it.

1. **Let a Spec state that its design is done.** v0 had a `designed` rung (01 §1.4, §2.5, §8
   stage 4); the Protocol has no way to say it. The adopter's owner asked for exactly this: "the
   Protocol should support Specs at any level of maturity, and that includes a Spec whose key
   abstractions are designed as stubs" (`libar-platform/docs/feedback/sdp-feedback-01.md`, "A Spec
   with designed stubs"). Its 792 Design entries, 653 of them with inline code, sit on Specs that
   read the same at `defined` whether their interfaces are settled or not. The code side landed in
   plan 40 (a stub `references` its design). Capture `spec:model.design-maturity` (kind `model`,
   rung `idea`) with one blocking question that weighs four answers: a fifth rung between `defined`
   and `ready`; a kind-conditional design clause on `ready` (MD-12 already allows one per rung); a
   derived design signal that is neither a rung nor a delivery fact; or a recipe only. Two laws
   shape it: the typing law (MD-11) closes the `design` section's shape the moment a floor reads
   it, and P8's corollary ("significance governs detail") argues against requiring design on every
   Spec.
2. **One recipe that lays v0's ladder out as independent columns.** Per Spec in a Pack or corpus:
   stated rung and floor reached, Design entries and pinned declarations, shaping decisions with
   their rungs, units that reference it, units that satisfy it, enabled verifiers, blocking
   questions. That is v0's `designed`, `bound`, `executable` read as derived columns with no
   ladder, which recipe 27 already set as the pattern. Today the answer is spread over recipes 5,
   9, 10, 24 and 27, and the adopter rebuilt it by hand in `scripts/pack-page.mjs`. It is cheap,
   needs no engine change, and is the reader evidence the adopter's own selection asks for before
   a rung is ruled. Home: `spec:consumers.agent-surface.design-ledger-recipe` (behavior, `scoped`).
3. **Give an open question an owner, links and a provisional reading.** v0's `OpenQuestion` had
   `owner` and `links` (01 §2.1); keys landed, the rest did not. The adopter keeps 175 decision
   rows in `design/decisions/register.json` beside the graph, each with who decides (class and
   advisor lens), sources, cites, a provisional reading the design proceeds on, and a lean. Most of
   that belongs on the question itself. Status fields (sorted, waiting, decided) must stay out under
   MD-2: a decided question leaves the Spec and its ruling lands as a decision Spec. Home:
   `spec:model.open-question-fields` (rule, `scoped`), refining `spec:model.open-question-keys`;
   `spec:consumers.spec-studio.spec-page#question.questionMetadata` is its first consumer.
4. **Spec-level change impact.** v0's North Star impact panel and `graph.impactOf(specId)` (00 §9,
   02 §1) ask what follows when a design changes. Blast radius answers for files; recipe 19 gives
   one hop. A recipe that walks inbound `refines`, `dependsOn`, `constrainedBy` and `decidedBy`
   transitively, then lists each dependent's referencing and satisfying units, verifiers, Packs and
   the prose that mentions it, is the "change to the design asks the code to follow" list MD-37
   implies. Home: `spec:consumers.agent-surface.design-impact-recipe` (behavior, `scoped`).
5. **A typed scoped-intent document as the hand-off.** v0's patch loop (02 §2, §4.2) made a
   proposed change reviewable and checked before it touched source. The Protocol refused the patch
   format, but the edit model's principle (`docs/concept/06` §4) sets its own re-entry bar: a
   structured edit contract when a second machine writer appears. The adopter now has several
   machine proposers: its four advisors each write a JSON patch to the decision register, and the
   main thread applies each lean to the Specs after a cross-model check
   (`libar-platform/design/advisors/README.md`, `register.md`). A typed intent document (scope by id or entry
   address, base commit, requested change, reason), checked by running `sdp validate` on a worktree
   before merge, keeps what v0 wanted without a codemod. It also answers the blocking
   `spec:consumers.spec-studio.intent-panel#question.handOff`. Home: mature
   `spec:consumers.intent-composition` (behavior, `idea` to `scoped`) and add a contract child
   (`contract`, `idea`).
6. **Component designs ahead of code, compared with the anchored architecture.** v0 named
   components and ports on the Spec (01 §2.5). MD-38 keeps a component that no code realizes yet as
   a Spec. Make that a convention: a component design Spec carries Design entries for its planned
   layer, context and uses, and a recipe compares them with the `component:` anchor once it
   exists (no anchor yet, layer or context mismatch, a `uses` edge the design does not name). The
   adopter states its component list and layer and context rules in prose (`design/PLAN.md` §2.1)
   and enforces isolation in `check.py`. Home: `spec:model.component-design` (model, `idea`) and a
   recipe child of `spec:consumers.agent-surface`. It stays inside MD-38, which refused a separate
   architecture file; the Spec is the design.
7. **A reason on a relation.** v0's relations carried a rationale, an aspect, a strength or a
   reason (01 §4). Today the why sits in narrative prose. On 2026-10-01 the adopter's mention audit
   (recipe 22) found 78 Spec pairs named in prose with no relation behind them, 50 with none in
   either direction (`sdp-feedback-01.md`); it now lists 12 as accepted warnings. MD-19 left
   edge-text ownership as a pending deliverable. Home: `spec:model.relation-rationale` (rule,
   `idea`), with the carrier change in `spec:carrier.markdown-authoring`.
8. **A decision register read from the graph.** v0 generated an ADR index (02 §1); the Protocol
   keeps `docs/concept/DECISIONS.md` by hand (numbers, curation, `held`, `superseded`), and the
   adopter keeps its own register. Decision Specs, `supersedes`, stated rungs and decided subjects
   are already graph data. A recipe that lists every decision with its rung (`defined` reads as
   held), its supersedes chain both ways, its subjects, its keyed open questions and its
   alternatives would replace most of the hand-kept table. Home:
   `spec:consumers.agent-surface.decision-register-recipe` (behavior, `scoped`).
9. **Keys on rules.** v0's `RuleSpec` had an optional id (01 §2.2). A rule entry has no address, so
   a review, a register row or an example cannot name the rule it is about
   (`spec:consumers.spec-studio.lenses#question.ruleCoverage`). The open-question key is the
   pattern. Home: `spec:model.rule-keys` (rule, `idea`), refining `spec:model.spec-sections`.
10. **Typed links to external design material.** v0 linked to Figma, Pencil, Storybook, diagrams and
    external ADRs (00 §7, 01 §2.5, §2.10). Today they would be untyped strings in an open bag, and
    `doc:` is a named deferral (MD-16). Home: `spec:model.external-design-links` (rule, `idea`),
    related to `spec:model.stable-ids`.

## Departures worth re-opening

Three of the departures rest on a `defined` Spec or a concept document rather than a `ready`
decision Spec: the four-rung ladder (`spec:model.core-model` and P8), the refusal of any structured
edit (`spec:consumers.edit-model` and `docs/concept/06` §4), and the dropped personas
(`jtbd-stories/README.md`). Re-opening any of them takes a new decision Spec that passes the ADR
test; nothing has to be superseded.

**A. The `designed` rung (re-open).** The other three dropped rungs should stay departed. `bound`
became `implemented`, and `executable` and `verified` became `has-verifier`; the adopter's evidence
supports that split. Nine of twenty mutation runs left a bound test green
(`sdp-feedback-01.md`, "What the build learned"), so a passed run would not have proved
`verified`. `designed` is different: it describes intent, not realization, so it does not collide
with D1. The evidence is the owner's request and the adopter's design passes, which needed a home
for "the key abstractions are settled" (`sdp-feedback-01.md`, items 1 to 3). Two cautions: the
adopter's own selection put "a `designed` rung" under "not now" until a reader pass shows the need
(`sdp-feedback-03-selection.md`), and P8 warns that a fixed tier invites padding. So capture it as
a question now (top recommendation 1), build the ledger recipe (2) as the cheap probe, and rule the
form after that.

**B. The patch loop, partly (re-open the hand-off, not the codemod).** v0's patch loop gave three
things: a proposed change you can review before it lands, a check before apply, and a guard against
a stale base. The edit model keeps the check, since every edit passes the same validators. It drops
the other two, because the agent edits source directly. Two pieces of evidence meet the re-entry
bar `docs/concept/06` §4 sets, a second machine writer: the Studio's composing panel needs a
hand-off format (blocking `#handOff`), and the adopter's advisors already hand the main thread
structured patches whose leans it applies to Specs. A
typed intent document with a base commit, validated on a worktree, gets back the reviewable,
pre-checked change without a patch schema or a codemod (top recommendation 5).

**C. Pack intent and descending constraints (hold).** v0 Packs carried intent and constraints that
applied to every member (01 §3). `spec:decisions.pack-reified` refuses this, and the adopter has
shown no case it could not express with framing plus each member's own `constrainedBy`. Keep the
ruling.

**D. Partial realization, v0's `bound` at entry grain (hold, watch).** MD-37 refused per-entry
`implemented` on 2026-10-10, unanimously. The cost is visible: at the adopter, 15 Specs moved from
`satisfies` to `references`, and `implemented` fell from 24 Specs to 9 (`pr-12-annotations-section.md`).
Recipe 27 still shows the difference: referenced but not satisfied. Keep the ruling while the
adopter tests the split path on the seven Specs it found with parts that could split. Re-open only
if those splits produce artificial child Specs.

**E. Components named on the Spec (no re-open).** MD-38's retained limitation is sound. A component
design Spec as a convention (top recommendation 6) recovers v0's design-ahead view without a second
place for the component to drift.
