# Decision registry — Libar Software Delivery Protocol

> The registry retains durable decision names, one-line glosses, and their carrying Specs. Historical execution and rationale live in git, plans, and the Specs themselves.

## The ratified-name registry

In prose, lead with meaning and use a code only as a parenthetical lookup key. Curation records the ADR three-part test: hard to reverse, surprising without context, and a real trade-off. `durable` marks a ruling in force; `folded` marks a historical ruling whose surviving law is carried by the Spec or code it points to. `held` marks a decision Spec that states `defined` and awaits the owner's `ready`; `superseded` marks a ruling a later entry replaces whole, and the row points forward. When the replaced record holds nothing a reader still needs, the replacing decision carries what survives and the record is deleted instead; its row stays, marked `deleted`, with a commit that holds its last text. A decision that narrows one part of another refines it, and the narrowed row names it.

Numbering is append-only: absent identifiers such as MD-3 and MD-6 are retired or reserved historical
positions and are never reused.
