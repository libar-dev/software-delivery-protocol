# The Protocol's roadmap

The Protocol's next work, first entry first. The order is a preference: it dates, gates and sequences nothing, and what each entry still needs is read from the graph each time.

First the cleanup that removes the frozen plans, review reports and superseded concept material, as the roadmap and the pull request record make possible. Then the two steps a reader of the Studio's pages could not take, the words of a test line that checks a declaration and an outside register's entries, and then the composed-intent model the build backlog holds. What the first adopter's mass update asks for joins when the owner selects it; a capture landing adds nothing here. An entry leaves by an edit to this manifest once its selected work is complete or no longer belongs among the next work.
