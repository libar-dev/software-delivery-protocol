## 1. Why this lens exists

PR #34's "Next" section asks the deep review to take the article packet and its seed / current / future distinctions as input. The other nine lanes are engineering checks: Specs against code, code defects, tests, documentation wording, and the inventory of old delivery records. None of them measures the product against the seed design.

The seed design is the original pre-code vision in `docs/lineage/v0-design`. This lens uses it as a **reference lens, never as intended truth**. A seed promise that the current product lacks is not a defect. It becomes a finding only when current intended truth (a Spec, a ruling, an article that claims to describe today) is wrong, silent where it says it decides, or missing a home for a direction the owner has named. Both verifiers held to this: seed aspirations create no current obligation, and a future direction in an article authorizes no implementation.

## 4. Confirmed findings

Eight confirmed: two design questions for the owner (one carrying a capture candidate), and six documentation corrections to PR #34's articles. Severity is the verifier's.

### Design questions for the owner

**L11-01 (medium). The AI presentation direction has no carrying question.** Part 5 names a real trust boundary: variable model-written prose against projections that are pure functions of the graph. No capture, question or deferral owns it. Following the verifier's correction, author it the way the corpus records other unbuilt directions (reference projection, runtime overlay, symbol reach): an idea-level capture with a blocking question, not a non-blocking question on the ready, implemented `spec:consumers.projections-model`.

- Spec address: `spec:consumers.ai-assisted-reading` (new capture; behavior; readiness idea)
- Outcome: "Let a reader ask for a useful existing reading and inspect why it was selected and what scope it omits."
- Question (blocking) `aiPresentationBoundary`: "If an assistant generates explanations or arrangements beyond selecting deterministic readings, what contract keeps its output distinct from graph claims and makes the exact reading reviewable?"
- Decider: owner
- Provisional: "Begin with selection of existing deterministic readings. Keep variable explanations outside the current Studio and the frozen projections; label them as assistant-generated and not byte-reproducible, keep the original claims on cited facts, expose source commit, scope and omissions, and decide how to retain the exact output with its model, prompt and named-input record. Add no claim category, delivery fact, or quality or workflow validator."
- Trigger: "The UX design pass measures that assistant selection or a generated explanation helps a named review task."
- Links: `docs/concept/01-founding-principles-and-invariants.md` (P3), `spec:consumers.projections-model`, `spec:extraction.claim-taxonomy`, `spec:consumers.spec-studio#rule.namedInputs`, `spec:consumers.context-bundle#rule.cutOrder`, `spec:consumers.review-perspectives#rule.contentIntact`, `spec:decisions.shipped-projections-frozen`, Part 5 at the article commit (lines 35-56 and 64-72).
- The capture adds no roadmap priority.

**L10-9 (low). The intent panel does not show the exact hand-off before Copy or Save.** Rows show scope and free-text request, but not the action ("Change target", "Propose example") or the exact text. The memo and proposal are readable only after copying or by opening the saved file. Exclusions are visible (the verifier narrowed the failure scenario accordingly). This is a design improvement, not a breach of the current Spec.

- Spec address: `spec:consumers.spec-studio.intent-panel`, question (non-blocking) `handOffPreview`
- Question: "Should the panel show the memo it will hand off, and each row's action and exact text, before Copy or Save?"
- Decider: owner
- Provisional: "Yes: a collapsed read-only memo preview plus the action label on each row; no new data."
- Links: `src/studio-elements/intent-panel-element.ts` (lines 129-130, 178-202), `docs/lineage/v0-design/07-spec-studio-and-projections.md` (pending-changes panel, 268-273), Part 2 question 6.
