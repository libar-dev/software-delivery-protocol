## The next work, from the roadmap

Roadmap: The Protocol's roadmap `pack:roadmap`

To state means the floor allows ready and a person still judges the statement. Built means every Spec of the entry's work states ready and has an implementation binding; it reports no passing test and no deployment.

The Protocol's next work, first entry first. The order is a preference: it dates, gates and sequences nothing, and what each entry still needs is read from the graph each time.

First the two steps a reader of the Studio's pages could not take, the words of a test line that checks a declaration and an outside register's entries, and then the composed-intent model the build backlog holds. What the first adopter's mass update asks for joins when the owner selects it; a capture landing adds nothing here. An entry leaves by an edit to this manifest once its selected work is complete or no longer belongs among the next work.
