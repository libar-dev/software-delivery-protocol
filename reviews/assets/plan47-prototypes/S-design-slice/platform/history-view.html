  • non-blocking · question.generationRollbackPolicy · spec:application.generation-registry · Extension E-8: the doc names a generation registry, a marker and a gate without their shapes, and says to keep the old generation for a rollback period without saying whether live commands keep writing it; the option taken here is the `generations` table below with seven states, a rollback period of 7 days, one generation being built per read model at a time, and a retired generation that is kept and not written, so that a rollback reopens it as `verifying`, one verify pass repairs what the writes since its retirement changed, and the ordinary switch makes it active again; a rollback is therefore a verify pass and then a switch of one write, never one write by itself (E-8, D9, Sc L2-5)
